From Detection to Prediction: Structural Break Forecasting in Commodity Prices

Working Paper — Walk-Forward Comparison of 16 Models

Author
Affiliation

Rodrigo Hermont Ozon

FAE Business School — PAIC

Published

February 22, 2025

Abstract

This page hosts the working paper “From Detection to Prediction: A Comparative Assessment of 16 Models for Structural Break, Peak, and Valley Forecasting in Agricultural Commodity Prices”. The paper evaluates 16 models across three methodological tiers using a strict walk-forward backtesting protocol on daily data for eight agricultural commodities (2019–2026). Key finding: 9 of 16 models produce zero predictive signals, demonstrating that retrospective detection does not imply predictive power. EVT, Conformal Prediction, and EEMD+LSTM emerge as the most promising tools for early warning systems.

Paper Overview

This working paper addresses a critical gap in the applied time series literature: the distinction between retrospective detection and prospective prediction of structural breaks in commodity prices.

Key Contributions

  1. Comprehensive walk-forward comparison of 16 models spanning classical changepoint methods, regime-switching/EVT approaches, and emerging ML/topological techniques
  2. Explicit quantification of the detection-prediction gap — most changepoint methods that work in full-sample fail entirely at prospective prediction
  3. Multi-commodity, multi-event evaluation across 8 agricultural commodities and 3 event types (structural breaks, peaks, valleys)

Main Findings

Finding Detail
Detection ≠ Prediction 9/16 models produce zero signals in walk-forward
Best for Valleys EVT (F1 ≈ 0.28, lead time ≈ 15 days)
Best for Peaks Conformal Prediction (F1 ≈ 0.28, high recall)
Best for Breaks WBS (F1 ≈ 0.26, but FAR > 80%)
F1 Ceiling No model exceeds F1 = 0.50
Actionable Lead Times 8–22 days advance warning achievable

Models Evaluated

NoteFull Model Universe (16 models, 3 tiers)

Tier 1 — Classical Changepoint (9 models): Bai-Perron, PELT, Binary Segmentation, CUSUM/ICSS, BCP, E-Divisive, WBS, BOCPD, tsoutliers

Tier 2 — Regime-Switching & EVT (4 models): Markov Switching, HMM, PLR+GBM, Extreme Value Theory

Tier 3 — Emerging Methods (4 models): TDA Persistent Homology, EEMD+LSTM, Conformal Prediction, Matrix Profile/FLUSS

## Paper Overview

This working paper addresses a critical gap in the applied time series literature: **the distinction between retrospective detection and prospective prediction of structural breaks in commodity prices.**

### Key Contributions

1. **Comprehensive walk-forward comparison** of 16 models spanning classical changepoint methods, regime-switching/EVT approaches, and emerging ML/topological techniques
2. **Explicit quantification** of the detection-prediction gap — most changepoint methods that work in full-sample fail entirely at prospective prediction
3. **Multi-commodity, multi-event evaluation** across 8 agricultural commodities and 3 event types (structural breaks, peaks, valleys)

### Main Findings

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Detection ≠ Prediction** | 9/16 models produce zero signals in walk-forward |
| **Best for Valleys** | EVT (F1 ≈ 0.28, lead time ≈ 15 days) |
| **Best for Peaks** | Conformal Prediction (F1 ≈ 0.28, high recall) |
| **Best for Breaks** | WBS (F1 ≈ 0.26, but FAR > 80%) |
| **F1 Ceiling** | No model exceeds F1 = 0.50 |
| **Actionable Lead Times** | 8–22 days advance warning achievable |
